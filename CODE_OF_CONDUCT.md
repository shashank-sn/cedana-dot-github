# Code of conduct

Cedana's repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions are a place to get work done together. This applies to everyone who takes part, including Cedana's own team.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people come with different experience, and help them get started.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeated disruption of a discussion after a maintainer asks you to stop.

## Report a problem

Write to contact@cedana.ai. Tell us what happened, where, and when. We keep reports private.

## What maintainers do

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break this code. They act on reports and tell the reporter what they did.
